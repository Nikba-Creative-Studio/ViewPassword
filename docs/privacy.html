ViewPassword

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 July 2026

Summary: ViewPassword does not collect, store, transmit, or sell any personal data. It has no servers and no network access. Everything happens locally, inside your browser.

1. Who we are

ViewPassword ("the extension") is a free, open-source browser extension developed by Nikba Creative Studio (www.nikba.com). This policy explains what the extension does — and does not do — with your information.

2. Data we collect

None. ViewPassword does not collect, log, or transmit any personal or usage data. Specifically, it does not:

  • send any data to us or to any third party;
  • use analytics, tracking, cookies, or advertising;
  • contain any remote code or external network requests;
  • read your browsing history or the content of pages you visit.

3. How the extension works

ViewPassword places a small toolbar next to password fields on web pages. When you explicitly click the copy or reveal button — or press the corresponding keyboard shortcut — the extension reads the value of that one password field solely to:

  • copy it to your device's clipboard, or
  • toggle it between hidden and visible on screen.

This value is used only at that moment and on your device. It is never stored by the extension and never leaves your browser.

4. Local storage

The extension saves a single preference locally — whether it is enabled or disabled — using the browser's local storage. This setting stays on your device and contains no personal information.

5. Permissions

The extension requests only the permissions required for its function:

Permission Why it is used
clipboardWrite Copy a password field's value to your clipboard when you ask.
storage Remember the enabled/disabled toggle locally.
activeTab Deliver a keyboard shortcut to the tab you are viewing.
Host access (all sites) Show the toolbar on password fields regardless of which site you are on. No page content is read or transmitted except a field's value at the moment you click copy or reveal.

6. What it cannot access

ViewPassword cannot read passwords stored inside your browser's encrypted password manager. It only works with the value currently loaded in a page's password field.

7. Third parties

We do not share, sell, or transfer any data to third parties, because the extension does not collect any data in the first place.

8. Children's privacy

The extension is a general-purpose utility and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
