ViewPassword

Enabled

Hover or focus any password field to show the toolbar:

  • Eye — show or hide the value
  • Copy — copy the value to the clipboard

Shortcuts (on a focused field):

  • Ctrl/Cmd + Shift + C — copy
  • Ctrl/Cmd + Shift + Y — show/hide

Runs 100% locally — no data ever leaves your browser.
